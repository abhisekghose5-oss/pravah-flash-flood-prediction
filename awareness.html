Western Ghats Monsoon Protocol • Public Advisory

PRAVAH Community Flood Awareness

Flash floods along the Western Ghats corridor can inundate river basins in less than 90 minutes. Review vital pre-flood safety measures, direct emergency contact hotlines, and designated high-ground evacuation sectors before monsoon cloudbursts strike.

Flash Inflow Window: < 90 mins
Danger Threshold: 120 mm / 24h
Active Shelters: 28 Relief Camps

Pre-Flood Preparation Steps

Proactive Protocols Before Flood Crest

When heavy rain alerts or ADVISORY thresholds are issued, take these life-saving measures immediately:

01

Pack a 72-Hour Waterproof Go-Bag

Prepare emergency cash, Aadhaar/deed documents in a sealed pouch, water purification tablets, 3 days of dry rations, and personal medications.

02

Relocate Cattle & Farm Equipment

Move livestock early to pre-designated highland community holding grounds before culverts and access causeways flood.

03

Clear Local Runoff Inlets & Ditches

Ensure farm perimeter trenches and neighborhood stormwater culverts are free of debris, fallen bamboo, and plastics.

04

Elevate Hazardous Utilities & Fuel

Turn off gas cylinders, disconnect solar inverters, and isolate main electrical panel breakers before leaving homes.

Evacuation Routes

Elevated Corridors & Staging Shelters

High-ground corridors designated by the District Disaster Management Authority to bypass submerged river bends:

Sector 1: Karad & Krishna Confluence Elev: 588m AMSL

Evacuate eastwards along NH-48 towards Vidyanagar elevation ridge. Avoid the Koyna-Krishna sangam causeway.

Assembly: Shivaji High School Relief Staging Ground
Sector 2: Mahad & Savitri Corridor Elev: 45m AMSL

Move towards Poladpur foothills via Old Mumbai-Goa Highway link roads. Do not cross low-lying Savitri bridges.

Assembly: Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar College Shelter
Sector 3: Kolhapur & Panchganga Basin Elev: 562m AMSL

Terwad & Kurundwad residents relocate to Shirol high school plateaus. Shirol-Jaysingpur bypass remains operational.

Assembly: Shirol Tehsil Community Relief Center

Golden Rules of Flood Survival

Follow these life-saving golden rules established by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA):

ZERO CASUALTY DIRECTIVE
Turn Around, Don't Drown

Just 15 cm (6 inches) of swiftly moving flood water can sweep an adult off their feet. 30 cm (12 inches) will float most passenger cars. Never attempt to wade, swim, or drive across flooded roads or submerged bridge culverts.

Electrocution Hazards

Avoid touching fallen power lines, submerged electrical poles, or flooded transformer sub-stations. Report sparkling cables immediately to MSEDCL emergency lines and keep a distance of at least 15 meters.

Official Information Channels

Rely exclusively on alerts issued by PRAVAH, the Central Water Commission (CWC), and District Collectorates. Do not forward unverified social media voice notes or panicking rumors during active cloudburst events.
